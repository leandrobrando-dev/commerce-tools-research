BMad Method · Planning Artifacts

The first commerce storefront that business users own, AI optimizes, and CFOs love — built natively for commercetools.

An AI-native, composable storefront platform that closes the structural gap in the commercetools ecosystem. Two layers: a storefront platform that marketing and merchandising teams own without developers, and an Autonomous Commerce Intelligence (ACI) layer that replaces $350K–$1M/yr in external analytics through native commerce-context awareness unavailable to any external tool.

Epic matrix · 7 epics, 65 FRs, 69 wireframe screens

Seven epics sequence the build. Two earlier epics dissolved into the current structure — Epic 7 (Migration) was first absorbed into Epic 4, then Epic 4 (AI Site Builder & Migrator) itself dissolved on 2026-05-12 — migration scope moved to Epic 1 Phase 1B/1C and AI Site Builder scope moved to Epic 3 Phase 3B. Click "Wireframes" to review the visual artifact or "Details" to read the epic narrative.

EPIC 01 · P0 · FOUNDATION + MIGRATION

Foundation, Migration & Operator Workspace

Tenant activation, commercetools project connection, SSO at procurement, data residency selection, the operator's daily workspace — plus brownfield migration paths (Frontastic, Custom Next.js, Monolith) absorbed from the dissolved Epic 4 on 2026-05-12. Sub-phases: 1A Scaffold (Stories 1.1–1.7) → 1B Migrator MVP (Stories 1.8–1.10, 1.13, 1.14) → 1C Migrator Phase 2 deferrable (Stories 1.11, 1.12). Epic 5 collection starts here, Epic 6 skeleton ships here.

Governed Component Library

EPIC 03 · P1 · CORE VALUE + AI BUILDER

Storefront Editor, Publishing & AI Site Builder

Business Operators visually build and edit storefronts on a populated canvas — starter template gallery, three-dimensional canvas (B2X × locale × consumer identity), behavioral analytics dissolved into the surface, multi-viewport preview, atomic deployment, immutable audit log, accessibility warnings before publish — plus the AI Site Builder warm-start completion engine absorbed from the dissolved Epic 4. Sub-phases: 3A Editor & Publishing (Stories 3.0–3.14) → 3B AI Site Builder (Stories 3.15–3.20). Commerce Intelligence Drawer in Create mode (inference-and-completion, not blank-canvas).

Reading paths — start here by role

24 artifacts is a lot. These paths are opinionated orderings for common roles — the fastest way to understand the project if you've just walked in.

"I need the story, the bet, and whether we're ready."
  1. Elevator pitch 2 min — the 30-second framing
  2. Product brief 15 min — the north star: problem, segments, thesis
  3. 12-month org chart 5 min — team + AI agent coverage · CEO view
  4. Epic matrix ↑ 5 min — the 8 bets, prioritized
  5. Readiness report 5 min — the green light

Product Manager / Designer

"I need to understand every decision and how they trace."
  1. Product brief 20 min — the thesis that shapes everything
  2. PRD 30 min — all 65 FRs and user journeys
  3. UX spec 25 min — defining experience + journeys + component strategy
  4. Design directions 10 min — the 6 options explored, and why Hybrid won

  6. Epic 8 wireframes 20 min — the moat made concrete
