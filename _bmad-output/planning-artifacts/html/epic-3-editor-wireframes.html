2 — Publish & Governance

The publish flow is the highest-stakes moment in the editor: it pushes changes to live production traffic. The UX must make it feel deliberate, informed, and safe — never a surprise. Pre-publish validation surfaces governance + accessibility issues before they reach customers. The audit log is immutable evidence of every publish.

Design Notes

  • Publish button copy counts the warnings — "Publish anyway (2 warnings)" forces acknowledgment of what's being accepted.

Design Notes

  • Immutable — rows cannot be edited or deleted, only appended. This is the compliance record. (FR20)
  • Human + AI actors both logged — AI agent appears as a distinct user with its own avatar; operators see full attribution.

3 — Multi-Viewport & B2X Preview

Operators need to see the effect of their changes across device sizes (desktop/tablet/mobile) and B2X rendering contexts (B2C/B2B/dealer) before publishing. Context preview is a primary operator affordance — never buried in settings. The B2XPreviewSplit component surfaces cross-context comparison as a deliberate pre-publish ritual.

Design Notes

  • All contexts visible at once — B2C (active + 2 drafts), B2B (live), Dealer (live). Operator instantly sees where work is pending. (FR5)
  • Per-context draft state preserved independently — switching to B2B doesn't discard B2C drafts. Core ContextSwitcher behavior.

Design Notes

  • Synchronized scrolling — as operator scrolls the left pane, right pane follows. Cross-context comparison becomes visceral. (B2XPreviewSplit component — Phase 2 per UX spec)
  • Change badges per component — green "+ added" for new, amber "Δ edited" for modified. Unchanged components have no badge — attention goes where changes live.
  • B2B and B2C use different components — B2C has TestimonialsCarousel, B2B has AccountDashboard and QuickOrder. Preview reflects the context schema, not just content.
  • Cross-context diff panel at the bottom — summarizes scope in plain language. The "Apply to B2B too" button makes cross-context broadcast a deliberate one-click action.
  • Red Zone rendering is context-aware — PricingRules shows "Consumer pricing" on B2C and "Wholesale pricing + MOQ" on B2B. Same component, different platform-managed behavior.
  • Publish button remains at the top — operators can publish directly from split view, not forced back to the editor. Reduces friction at the decision moment.
Wireframes are intentionally low-fidelity — dashed borders and placeholder content indicate where customer-brand content will render. Final visual styling inherits commercetools Merchant Center design tokens.
